## 🎯 1. Executive Summary: What Are We Actually Building?

We are building **Zonek (Bridgehead)**, an un-hallucinated, hyper-local business feasibility and execution platform designed for startup founders, retail operators, and local entrepreneurs.

### The Problem We Solve:
Most new businesses don't fail from bad ideas—they fail from **blind execution and fragmented local data**:
1. **The Blind Capital Gamble:** Founders risk life savings on 3-year commercial leases without knowing realistic footfall, break-even unit economics, or local customer spend.
2. **Fragmented Ground Reality:** Rental rates are hidden with brokers, competitor prices must be checked on foot, and government subsidies (Mudra, PMEGP) are buried in bureaucratic portals.
3. **The "Generic AI" Trap:** Existing AI tools (like ChatGPT) give generic textbook fluff (*"post on social media"*, *"provide good service"*). Zonek gives micro-location reality (*"In Indiranagar, 600 sqft costs ₹75,000/mo; 68% gross margin needed; 4 direct competitors exist; here are your 4 required licenses"*).
4. **The Execution Void:** Founders lack a Chief Operating Officer (COO) to guide them on what to do in **Week 1 vs. Week 6**.

## 🌐 2. The 8 Core Product Pillars

| # | Pillar | What Exists Today | What Happens Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Founder Command Dashboard** | Basic route placeholder in App.tsx | Dual-state UI: Launchpad for new visitors, Active 90-day launch command center with checkable sprint tasks & budget burn meter for returning founders. |
| **2** | **Feasibility Research Engine** | Monolithic `BusinessResearch.tsx` with fast `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (2–4s per section) & Supabase DB integration | Split into modular **5-Tab Sub-Navbar** (`Overview`, `Location & Rentals`, `Competitors & Longevity`, `Financials`, `Gov Schemes`) + 1-Click Investor PDF Download. |
| **3** | **Demands Marketplace** | `Demands.tsx` displaying community unmet business requests | Add 1-click **"Analyze This Opportunity"** button to auto-populate the Research Engine. |
| **4** | **Commercial Rentals** | `Rentals.tsx` displaying commercial spaces | Link active rental listings dynamically into the research reports as verified site options. |
| **5** | **Gov Data ("For The People")** | `GovData.tsx` viewing civic metrics | Polish civic transparency portal for public subsidies (Mudra, PMEGP), weather, and mandi prices. |
| **6** | **AI Idea Generator** | Planned | Build AI generator that produces 3–5 viable business concepts matched to real local unmet demands. |
| **7** | **Founder Community** | Legacy chat/forum code | Build clean, lightweight **Founder Discussion Board** on Supabase (licensing, vendors, locations). |
| **8** | **Network Messaging** | Legacy Socket.io prototype | Build clean **1-on-1 Direct Messaging** for founders to contact landlords and partners. |
